🛡️ Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

1. Financial Data Ownership & Zero Cloud Tracking

UPI Spendr operates strictly with local storage and client-side database models (upi_tracker.db). We never sell, transmit, or monetize your bank account numbers, SMS logs, or UPI PINs to third-party advertising networks.

2. Security & UPI Standard Compliance

All UPI links are built using the official NPCI Specification (upi://pay). PIN verification in the simulator is purely sandboxed and never transmits real banking credentials.

3. Data Export & Right to Erasure

You maintain full control over your financial records. You can download a complete CSV or JSON backup of your database, or wipe all records permanently with one click.
